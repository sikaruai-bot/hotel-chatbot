# Staff & Owner Operational Manual

Welcome to the **Hotel Sherpa Soul Staff CRM & Chatbot Dashboard**! This manual is designed for the hotel owner and front desk team. You do not need any programming knowledge to manage conversations, update prices, or take over chats.

---

## 1. Opening the Staff Dashboard

1. Open your browser on any phone, tablet, or computer.
2. Go to: **`http://localhost:3000/admin/inbox`** (or your live CRM link).
3. The dashboard has 5 main sections accessible from the top navigation bar:
   - 💬 **Inbox**: All live guest chats across WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, and Website.
   - 👥 **Lead CRM**: Pipeline of guests interested in booking.
   - 🛏️ **Rooms & PMS**: 6-room inventory and live rate changes.
   - 📖 **Knowledge Base**: Change hotel answers, FAQs, policies, and booking links.
   - 📊 **Analytics**: View daily inquiries, popular rooms, and conversion rates.
   - 💻 **Meta Simulator**: Test scenarios without needing real phones.

---

## 2. Managing Conversations (The Inbox)

### How to Tell Who is Inquiring
- On the left side, you will see a list of guest chats.
- Each conversation shows the guest's name, their channel (WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram, or Website), and the time of their last message.
- A **red dot** means the guest sent a message that needs front-desk attention.

### Intent Badges
- 🔥 **HIGH INTENT** (Gold badge): The guest provided travel dates, number of guests, or asked to book. *Prioritize these conversations immediately!*
- ⚠️ **HUMAN** (Rose badge): The guest asked to speak to a person, has a complaint, wants airport pickup, or asked something unusual.

### Taking Over a Conversation from the Bot
1. Click on the guest's conversation.
2. In the top right corner of the chat, click the purple **"Take Over"** button.
3. The status will change to **👤 Staff Mode**.
4. The bot will automatically stop replying.
5. Type your message in the bottom box and press **Enter** or click the **Send** button. Your message goes directly to the guest's WhatsApp, Messenger, or Instagram!

### Resuming the Chatbot
When you are done answering the guest's specific question:
1. Click the green **"Resume Bot"** button.
2. The bot will take back over automated inquiries for that guest.

### Using Quick Replies
Above the message input box, you will find 1-click **Quick Reply** buttons:
- **Room Prices**: Pastes current rates.
- **No Restaurant Info**: Explains our peaceful sleep philosophy.
- **Location & Taxi**: Provides directions and offers airport pickup assistance.
- **Shared Kitchen**: Explains Room 102 rules for stays of 2 weeks or more.
- **Check-in Policy**: Check-in at 12:00 PM (noon), check-out at 11:00 AM.

## 3. How to Change Room Prices (No Code Needed!)

If you want to adjust prices for high season or low season:
1. Click **Rooms & PMS** in the top navigation bar.
2. Under **Active Room Rates**, find the room type:
   - Deluxe Room
   - Budget Family Room
   - Family Room
3. Type the new price in the USD box (e.g., change `20` to `25`).
4. Click **Update**.
5. *Done!* The chatbot immediately quotes the new price to all future guest inquiries.

---

## 4. Understanding Our Room Inventory Rules

- **Rooms 201, 202, 203** (Second Floor) — Sellable guest rooms.
- **Rooms 301, 302, 303** (Third Floor) — Sellable guest rooms.
- **Room 101** — Does **not** exist.
- **Room 102** — **Shared Kitchen Facility** for guests staying at least 2 weeks (14 nights). It is **not** a guest bedroom and **not** a public restaurant.

---

## 5. Adding New Hotel Facts or FAQs

1. Go to **Knowledge Base** in the top menu.
2. Click **+ Add Knowledge Item**.
3. Choose a category (e.g., `FAQ`, `POLICIES`, `TRANSPORT`).
4. Type the question guests ask and your approved answer.
5. Click **Save Knowledge Item**.
6. The chatbot will instantly use your answer when guests ask about that topic.
